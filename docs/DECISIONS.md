| 2026-10-03 | Review M2: `_releaseReserve` deletes the market's schedule through `HSS.deleteSchedule` when a settlement or void happens before the run, ignoring the code; `m.schedule` stays on the market as a record. | Releasing the reserve while the run was still pending let a later run be charged to Verdict after a sweep, which could take the balance below collateral plus pending reserves (invariant 1). |
| 2026-10-03 | Review M3: `ChainlinkResolver.readingAt` is a binary search over the aggregator's current phase (round ids within a phase are contiguous, `updatedAt` never decreases) for the greatest round at or before the time, bounded by `MAX_READS` 40 reads, every read in `try`. A failed read or a search the cap stopped before converging reads as no fresh reading. | A linear walk capped at 32 rounds lost the reading whenever more than 32 rounds were published after expiry before anyone resolved, and the losing side could void at 0.5 with a fresh round at expiry on record. 40 reads cover any phase shorter than 2^40 rounds and bound the gas. |
| 2026-10-03 | Review L1: `createMarket` reverts `InvalidBounds` unless the bounds it keeps fit in `int128` (`lower` for every kind, `upper` for Between and Scalar). | `payoutFor(Scalar, -1e70, 1e70, 1e8)` panics on the interpolation, and a market stored with such bounds could never resolve, be resolved by its schedule, or void. |
| 2026-10-03 | Review L2: the expiry second itself is not yet expired. `resolve` and `resolveScheduled` require `block.timestamp > expiry`, and the schedule is placed at the first second after expiry with capacity. `split` still closes from the expiry second, so for that one second a market is neither open nor settleable. | The round current at expiry is only final once that second has passed; settling inside it could settle on a reading a later transaction in the same second supersedes. |
| 2026-10-03 | Review L3: the creation charge is measured from before the token creations to after `_schedule`, and `createMarket` reverts `InsufficientValue(charge, msg.value)` when the measured charge exceeds what was sent. `creationCost()` stays the upfront estimate. | The old measurement covered token creation only and was never compared with `msg.value`; an HSS charge at scheduling time would have been absorbed by the contract. No mock can take more HBAR than the value sent with a call, so the revert is unreachable locally and guards the real HSS. |
| 2026-10-03 | Review L4: `quoteSellNo` returns `(hbarNeeded, noIn - hbarNeeded)` and `sellNo` bounds that net with `minHbarOut`; the caller is still paid `noIn` plus the unspent part of `msg.value`, and `Traded` is unchanged. The `IVerdictRouter` NatSpec says net; the ABI did not change. | The old bound compared `noIn + refund` with `minHbarOut`, which never fired because the refund is the caller's own money; the quote reported the gross for the same reason. |
| 2026-10-03 | Review L5: `_schedule` probes each of the `MAX_SCHEDULE_PROBES` seconds after expiry through a `try` around `hasScheduleCapacity`, attempts no `scheduleCall` when none has capacity (`ScheduleFailed` carries the busy code 370), and the reserve is charged and counted in `pendingReserves` only when a schedule exists; otherwise it comes back in the creator's refund. | The eighth second was scheduled blind, a reverting probe would have reverted creation, and a market with no run to pay for was still charged 5 HBAR. |
| 2026-10-03 | The HSS mock charges nothing for a scheduled run and makes the call from `0x16b`; the real sender and the fee at execution are spike 2's to confirm. The M2 test simulates the execution charge with `hardhat_setBalance` after the run. | A contract cannot take HBAR from another, so a mock cannot charge a payer; the test shows invariant 1 holds because the reserve is held until the run. |
| 2026-10-03 | Outcome tokens name Verdict as their auto-renew account with a 7,890,000 second period; renewal charging is off on Hedera today, so nothing is reserved for it. | If renewal charging is turned on, the charge lands on Verdict's balance like a scheduled run's fee and is covered the same way, by surplus above collateral and pending reserves. |
| 2026-10-03 | `RESOLVE_GAS` stays at 2,000,000 and `RESOLUTION_RESERVE` stays a constant. On the mocks the scheduled run uses at most 181,092 gas (gas reporter, 2026-10-03); on testnet the worst case adds up to 40 cold aggregator reads through the feed proxy, roughly 12,000 gas each, plus the `deleteSchedule` call whose cost spike 2 will measure, so about 700,000 at the limit. Hedera bills at least 80 percent of the limit, which at 2,000,000 is about 1.1 HBAR a run at today's gas price, inside the 5 HBAR reserve. | A failed scheduled run during judging costs a manual `resolve`; halving the limit saves about half an HBAR a market against a 1.4 times margin on the worst case with one cost still unmeasured. Revisit after spike 2 with the measured delete cost; making the reserve owner-settable for new markets is a small change (`m.reserve` is already per market) that is not needed until then. |
| 2026-10-03 | `redeem` rounds each leg down separately: up to 1 tinybar is left behind per leg, so up to 2 per call, and it stays as the market's collateral. | Rounding always favours the contract, which is what invariant 3 needs; the property test and the rounding test assert redemptions never exceed the collateral. |
